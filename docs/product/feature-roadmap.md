# AI School OS — Feature Roadmap

## Roadmap Overview

### Completed Phases
- **Phase 1 – Phase 19**: Core Architecture, Master Data, Academics, Timetable, Exams & Grading, Fee Management, Student Promotion Engine, RBAC & Tenant Isolation Hardening.
- **Phase 20**: Login Rate Limiting & Production Security Hardening.
- **Phase 21**: Product Feature Gap Audit.
- **Phase 22**: P0 Core ERP Completion (Teacher Attendance Workstation, Transfer Certificate & Bonafide Certificate Generation).
- **Phase 23**: Homework & Assignments Module (Complete workflow: teacher assignment, student text submission, teacher grading, parent child tracking, in-app notifications, RBAC denial, cross-tenant isolation).
- **Phase 24**: Secure Student & Staff Document Management (Private filesystem storage, magic byte signature validation, SHA-256 checksums, version history, verify/reject status, relationship authorization, Accountant denial, tenant isolation, full audit logging).

### Planned Future Phases
- **Phase 25**: Payment Gateway & Online Fee Collection (Unscheduled).
- **Phase 26**: Reception CRM & Visitor Management (Unscheduled).
- **Phase 27**: SMS/WhatsApp Notifications (Unscheduled).
